# Blackjack - 21

A classic Blackjack (21) card game implemented in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Play against the dealer and try to get as close to 21 as possible without going over!

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Click "New Game" to start a new round
3. You'll be dealt two cards, and the dealer gets two cards (one hidden)
4. Click "Hit" to draw another card
5. Click "Stand" to end your turn and let the dealer play
6. Try to get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over!

## Rules

- Number cards (2-10) are worth their face value
- Face cards (J, Q, K) are worth 10 points
- Aces are worth 11 points (or 1 if that would cause you to bust)
- The dealer must hit on 16 or less and stand on 17 or more
- Getting 21 with your first two cards is a Blackjack!
- Going over 21 is a bust and you lose

- 🎴 Realistic card design with suits
